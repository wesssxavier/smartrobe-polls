Delete your account or your data

This page explains how to delete your Smartrobe account, or only some of your data, and what happens to it. It applies to the Smartrobe app on the App Store and Google Play.

Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Smartrobe and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@smartrobe.com from the email address of your Smartrobe account, with the subject "Delete my Smartrobe account". If you signed in with Apple or Google, use the email address of that Apple or Google account, or tell us which one you used. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address, and we complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, name, sign-in details) and profile (gender, sizes, style preferences, city).
  • Your wardrobe: photos of your clothes and every detail about them.
  • Outfits, outfit calendar, notes, trips, packing lists, wishlist, polls and their votes.
  • Photos of you: Look Check photos, avatar and try-on images, and outfit photos.
  • Your messages to the stylist, usage history and notification settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own rules. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
  • Copies in routine backups are removed as those backups expire.
  • Our AI providers may keep the data from recent requests for up to 30 days to prevent abuse, then delete it.
  • We keep nothing else, unless the law requires us to.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

In the app

You can delete any piece of clothing, photo, outfit, planned look, trip, poll or wishlist item at any time from its page in the app. It is removed from our systems straight away.

By email

To have other specific data removed (for example your avatar photos, your outfit photos or your messages to the stylist), email support@smartrobe.com from your account's email address and tell us what to delete. We complete requests within 30 days.
